THE DESIGNER

“Good icons should be more like road signs than illustrations.”

Trained as a fine artist, Susan Kare joined Apple’s Macintosh team in the early 1980s. Working on graph paper, she created the icons and typefaces that made computing feel clear, direct, and personal.

Her icons, cursors, and bitmap typefaces became defining features of the original Mac. She later designed for NeXT, Microsoft, and Pinterest—always distilling complex ideas into approachable, highly legible forms.
